Design the core loop's feel: the grind state, live hashrate, the improvement log as candidates get better, leading-zero emphasis on the best hash, and the epoch countdown expressed in blocks rather than minutes.

## Done when

- A full grind session reads coherently from idle through improvement to stop.
- Leading zeros are visually distinguished from the rest of the digest.
- The screen states plainly that a found candidate proves no entitlement.
