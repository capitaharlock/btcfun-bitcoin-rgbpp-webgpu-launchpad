Review the proposed paid-ticket/reward product, operating jurisdictions, disclosures, economics and operational readiness before any real-fund rollout.

## Execution

- Phase: 4.
- Prerequisites: `SH5`, `SH6`, `PV3`.

## Done when

- Record qualified assessment of the paid participation/reward model and applicable operating constraints; do not assume a classification.
- User-facing terms accurately describe variable payouts, reserve denomination, fees, escrow, finality and exit limits.
- All technical, independent-review, operational and product gates have evidence and unresolved launch blockers are absent.
- Record an explicit go/no-go and staged rollout limits. No mainnet deployment is performed by this planning task.
