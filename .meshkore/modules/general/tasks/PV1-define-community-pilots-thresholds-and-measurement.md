Prepare the initial community segment and research plan early; integrated pilots begin only after the demo gate. Define the smallest UX experiments that can run before contracts exist.

## Execution

- Phase: 0–3.
- Prerequisites: `V0`.

## Done when

- Specify recruitment, consent, measurement denominators and numeric pass/fail thresholds before pilots.
- Measure first-cycle completion, repeat participation, economic comprehension, concentration and net operating margin.
- Document BRO as a precedent and test the complete launch/verification value proposition rather than mining novelty.
- Separate usability evidence, willingness-to-pay evidence and real-fund authorization; testnet is not proof of paid demand.
