# Community and business validation

## Why this exists

Browser mining already has precedents. Demand for community launches with verification and reserves must be demonstrated; testnet participation alone is not paid demand.

## Execution and gate

PV1 defines audience, small early UX experiments and numeric thresholds before pilots. PV2 requires the Phase 2 demo evidence, including PC7 and operator/reorg recovery. PV3 decides whether to proceed. No real funds before Phase 4.

Gate order and shared evidence: [roadmap](../../docs/roadmap.md).

## Done when

- Pilots report completion, repeat behavior, comprehension, concentration and fully costed operating assumptions.
- Results distinguish usability from willingness to pay and identify subsidy dependence.
- A go/narrow/pivot/demo-only decision cites predeclared thresholds; failure blocks commercial expansion.
